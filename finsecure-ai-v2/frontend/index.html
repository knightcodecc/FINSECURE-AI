Why Do the Thresholds Differ?

1. Cost-Optimal Threshold (T* = 0.0484): Minimizes direct rupee loss. False Negatives cost the full transaction rupee amount (amount × 1.0), whereas False Positives incur only a fixed analyst investigation cost (Rs. 50.0). Because single fraud transactions can exceed Rs. 1,000,000, minimizing financial loss demands flagging transactions at a lower probability.

2. F2-Optimal Threshold (T_F2 = 0.4094): Optimizes statistical balance. It weights Recall 4 times as heavily as Precision, but treats every transaction as having equal value regardless of rupee magnitude.

Operational Decision: FinSecure AI v2 deploys the Cost-Optimal threshold to prevent severe financial bleed.
